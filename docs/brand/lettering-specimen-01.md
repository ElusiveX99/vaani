# Lettering specimen 01 — construction studies

**Status:** Uploaded exploratory drawing · not an approved wordmark · not production-ready.

This sheet is the first controlled drawing pass after the research foundation. It tests three different structural approaches to the exact word **Vaani**. These are vector-drawn construction studies, not font samples and not claims of a completed bespoke identity.

## What is being tested

1. **Continuous gesture:** one repeated, controlled rhythm across the word. The V's entry and descent are intended to lead into the lowercase forms without a separate underline or ornamental tail.
2. **Editorial construction:** a more measured baseline with geometric bowls and consistent stems. This is the legibility baseline against which more expressive work should be judged.
3. **Pressure and pause:** a limited contrast study in which the V carries more weight. This deliberately tests—and exposes—the risk of allowing the initial to overpower the name.

## Initial critique

- **Study 01:** best potential for a coherent signature rhythm, but the monoline treatment is still too generic. Its repeated bowl construction needs more authored character, and the lowercase connections are not yet a distinctive system.
- **Study 02:** strongest legibility and most stable proportions. At present it reads as a neutral geometric lettering exercise rather than a recognizable Vaani identity.
- **Study 03:** the contrast is controlled, but the V dominates. It repeats the old failure mode in which the initial becomes a separate logo and the rest of the name becomes secondary.

## Decision

Do not advance any of these as a logo. Study 01 is the most useful construction to develop, while Study 02 remains the legibility control. Study 03 should not be developed further unless the V's visual weight can be brought into a coherent relationship with the lowercase letters.

No abstract symbol is included. It would be premature to derive one before the wordmark has a distinctive, approved gesture.

## Tests still outstanding

- Compare the actual silhouette against relevant contemporary and cultural identity references.
- Refine the repeated **a** forms so they are related but not mechanically duplicated.
- Resolve V–a and n–i spacing optically.
- Test at 16 px, 24 px, 32 px, and realistic header sizes.
- Test reverse, one-color print, and embroidery.
- Complete the evidence-based 50-point evaluation; no numerical score is assigned while critical gates remain untested.

**Important:** The current drawings are an honest first specimen, not finished craft. The next pass must materially improve letterform authorship and distinctiveness before any symbol, color, or production asset is considered.
