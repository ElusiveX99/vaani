# Vaani brand identity — Charan Kamal

The identity is inspired by **Shri Radha Charan Kamal**. It is an abstract devotional emblem, not a literal depiction of sacred feet.

## Design construction
- The outer bloom is a restrained lotus silhouette, drawn as a balanced family of petals.
- Paired inner negative spaces subtly suggest Charan without illustrating feet literally.
- A fine antique-gold center detail adds warmth without ornamental excess.
- The same master geometry is used for the primary, dark, monochrome, favicon, app-icon, and social-preview artwork.

## Palette
- Deep plum: `#593B53`
- Muted rose: `#AD7D83`
- Soft petal: `#E7D4D0`
- Warm ivory: `#FFFDF9`
- Antique gold: `#C5A07D`

## Assets
- `vaani-emblem.svg` — master emblem for light surfaces.
- `vaani-emblem-light.svg` — contrast-adjusted artwork for dark surfaces.
- `vaani-emblem-monochrome.svg` — single-ink variant.
- `vaani-wordmark.svg` — standalone wordmark.
- `vaani-lockup.svg` — horizontal master lockup.
- `favicon.svg` — square icon derived from the same master paths.
- `/icon` and `/apple-icon` — high-resolution PNG app icons.

All supplied SVGs use vector paths and text; no raster artwork is embedded.
